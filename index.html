The Boyband
Blueprint

Some bands wore one look. Others wore five. This dial measures how tightly each group enforced a visual formula — and opens the record when a member breaks formation. Ring length shows repetition, wedge depth shows band size; the era pattern is secondary, not the headline.

How to read the dial

A four-move reading path, from aggregate to individual.

  1. 01

    Depth = band size

    Every group owns one wedge. The further its arc reaches out, the more members it has (3–7).

  2. 02

    Colour = metric, fill = repetition

    From outside in: crimson hair colour, amber hair length, teal shirt colour, green trouser colour. A full ring means everyone documented matched. Quarter ticks mark 25, 50 and 75%.

  3. 03

    Dots = one member

    Dot hue is hair colour; area is height. A dark hollow ring means no height is listed. Click a wedge to lift the slice and read its people.

  4. 04

    Marker = confidence

    A solid marker means five or more members. An open marker and “low confidence” flag the coarser 3–4-member scores.
